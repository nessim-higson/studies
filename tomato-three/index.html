tomato.three

The third Tomato CD-ROM, London, 1999 — a Macromedia Director title pressed for Mac and never ported. This is its contents recovered off the disc: the archive of 34 projects in the running order the disc itself used, the showreel, the spoken piece, and the interface typography lifted back out of the Director casts.

the archive

The disc kept its gallery as a Lingo property list inside book.dxr — every plate tagged with its project and whether it opened one. That list is what orders this page, so the sequence below is the disc's own.

the spoken piece 16 parts

Sixteen clips, one continuous text. The words were on the disc as bitmaps, not as type — recovered here from the cast of spoken.dxr and transcribed.

sound pieces 4

Four QuickTime files that carry a one-pixel dummy video track and nothing but audio underneath — sound pieces, not films.

the interface recovered from the casts

Every word of the interface was drawn as an 8-bit bitmap. These are those bitmaps, decompressed out of the Director casts and shown at 2×.

How this was read. A .dxr is a protected Director movie: the Lingo source is stripped, but the cast survives. The file is a big-endian RIFX container — its imap points at an mmap that lists every chunk, and KEY* ties each cast member to its data.

Text came out of STXT; names out of the CASt info lists; the artwork out of BITD, which is a byte-run RLE over indices into the classic Mac system palette. The tools that did it are in tools/.

The media was transcoded rather than rewrapped — the video is Sorenson SVQ1 and the audio is QDM2 and IMA ADPCM, none of which a browser will decode.
