The page is a card that hasn't been revealed yet.

A rebuild of the page→card scroll move from marvinschwaibold.com. This white page never resizes — as its bottom edge climbs the viewport, a clip-path carves the sides away and rounds the corners until the whole page reads as a card floating on black. Scroll.

How it works

clip-path: inset(0 X round R) the entire visual, one property
1 − bottom / viewportHeight the scroll driver
smoothstep + onset delay gentle start, gentle end
lerp toward target each frame the scrub lag that sells it
content never moves no reflow — edges just clip it
